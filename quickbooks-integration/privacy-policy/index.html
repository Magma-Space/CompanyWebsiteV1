Privacy Policy
QuickBooks Online Integration

This page describes the privacy practices of MagmaSpace's internal QuickBooks Online integration ("the App"). The App is a private, internal tool used solely by Magma Space Inc. to synchronize its own QuickBooks Online accounting data into MagmaSpace's private internal database for internal financial reporting and analysis. The App is not offered to the public and is not available for use by any other company or individual.

Data accessed: The App accesses QuickBooks Online data belonging to Magma Space Inc.'s own company file, including but not limited to accounts, classes, customers/projects, vendors, and transactions.

Data storage and use: Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is stored in a private database controlled by Magma Space Inc. and is used exclusively for Magma Space Inc.'s internal financial reporting. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party, except as necessary to operate the integration itself (for example, the data pipeline service used to perform the synchronization, and the cloud infrastructure provider hosting the database).

Data security: Access to the underlying database is restricted to authorized Magma Space Inc.'s personnel and is not publicly accessible.

Contact: Questions about this policy may be directed to j.handler@magma-space.com.
